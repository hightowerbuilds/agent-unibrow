Kestrel Outfitters: quarterly report

This report covers the four quarters of the year for the Kestrel Outfitters store network.

Store traffic was steady through spring. The summer catalog launched late but recovered by August.

Inventory turnover improved, helped by the new warehouse in the north region.

Staffing levels were unchanged. Training hours rose slightly over the previous year.

Customer satisfaction scores held at the same level as last year across all regions.

Returns fell for the second year running, mostly in footwear and outerwear.

The loyalty program added members in every quarter, with the most in the autumn.

Online orders now make up a growing share of sales; store pickup is the most popular option.
