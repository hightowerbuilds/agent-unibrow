agent-unibrow

A browser you can ask to do things.

Ask the agent

Type in the chat on the right (Alt+1). Some things to try:

  • “Find a bone jazz bass nut under $15 and show me the best rated one.”
  • “Watch this page and tell me when the price drops.”
  • “Summarize the article I have open in three bullets.”
  • “What did I look at on sweetwater last week?”

Make it yours

  • ModelPick the agent and model with the Model button at the bottom of the sidebar.
  • Focus sites◎ Focus in the toolbar marks a site. Focus (Alt+3) lists them, and Learn with Jev answers questions about them without Claude.
  • History and TraceAlt+2 shows what you visited, your bookmarks, and every step the agent took.
  • ExplainedAlt+5 is the guide to everything in the app.
